Franchise Partners

Two of the strongest brand families in the business.

Every AHM hotel flies a national flag. Bookings come in through Marriott Bonvoy and Choice Privileges, which takes a lot of the guesswork out of demand compared with an independent hotel. The brands' renovation requirements (PIPs) also keep us from putting off capital work.
