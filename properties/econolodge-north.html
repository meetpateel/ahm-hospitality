About this property

The hotel sits at 5100 N Cliff Avenue on the north side of Sioux Falls, 2.3 miles from Sioux Falls Regional Airport and a short drive from I-90. It serves leisure travelers driving through, families in town for regional and youth sports tournaments, and business travelers watching their budget.

Rooms have flat-screen TVs, refrigerators, coffee makers and a work desk. Non-smoking and family-configured rooms are available.

There's an indoor pool, a fitness room, continental breakfast, free Wi-Fi and free parking, and pets are welcome. AHM's own team runs the hotel, which books through Choice Privileges. Downtown, the Denny Sanford Premier Center and the industrial employers on the north side are all a short drive away.
